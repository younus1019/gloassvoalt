A Few Things Worth Knowing.

How do I book a detailing appointment?

Submit an enquiry through our contact form with your vehicle details and preferred service. Our team can discuss your requirements and help arrange an appointment.

Which detailing service is right for my car?

It depends on your vehicle's condition and your goals. Interior detailing focuses on cabin cleanliness, paint correction addresses suitable paint imperfections, and ceramic coating or PPF can provide different forms of paint protection. Contact us to discuss your options.

How long does a detailing service take?

Timing varies with the service selected, vehicle size, and condition. We'll give you an estimated timeframe after understanding your vehicle and treatment requirements.

What is the difference between ceramic coating and PPF?

Ceramic coating adds a protective surface layer that helps with gloss, water repellency, and maintenance. Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a physical film designed to help protect paint from certain stone chips and surface impacts. The right option depends on your priorities and budget.

Should I book an appointment before visiting?

We recommend contacting the studio before visiting so your service requirements and appointment availability can be discussed in advance.

Can I get a quote before choosing a service?

Yes. Share your vehicle model, the service you're considering, and any specific concerns through the enquiry form. The team can discuss suitable options and pricing with you.
