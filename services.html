THE ART OF PAINT REFINEMENT

Reveal the True Finish.

Swirl marks, light scratches, and dull paint can affect the clarity of your vehicle's finish. Paint correction refines suitable paintwork through a controlled polishing process.

BEFORE — PAINT CONDITION Surface imperfections
AFTER — REFINED FINISH Enhanced clarity and gloss

Results depend on the paint condition, defect depth, and available clear-coat thickness. Not every imperfection can safely be removed.
