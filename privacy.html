01

THE BASICS

Privacy, with clarity.

This Privacy Policy describes how the GLOSSVAULT website may collect, use, store, and disclose information when you visit the site, submit a form, or contact us. It is intended as a starter policy for the website and should be reviewed against the actual services, tools, and legal requirements that apply to your business.

By using the website, you acknowledge that information may be handled as described here. If you do not agree with these practices, please do not submit personal information through the website.

02

INFORMATION

Information we may collect.

Contact details

Information such as your name, email address, phone number, and details you choose to provide in a booking or enquiry form.

Service details

Information about your vehicle, requested service, preferred appointment, or other details needed to respond to your enquiry.

Device information

Technical information such as browser type, device characteristics, pages viewed, and general usage data may be processed by hosting or analytics tools.

Messages you send

Questions, feedback, or other information included in messages you send to us.

Please only provide information that is relevant to your enquiry. Do not submit payment card details or other sensitive information through a general contact form.

03

PURPOSE

How information may be used.

  • Respond to questions, service enquiries, and booking requests.
  • Coordinate appointments and communicate about requested services.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the website and user experience.
  • Help protect the website against spam, fraud, misuse, or security incidents.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

DISCLOSURE

When information may be shared.

GLOSSVAULT does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with service providers that support website hosting, form delivery, appointment management, security, or analytics; where you ask us to share it; or where disclosure is required by law or needed to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the service.

Only list providers that your website actually uses. Update this section if you add booking, payment, analytics, advertising, or customer-management integrations.

SITE TECHNOLOGY

Cookies and analytics.

The website or its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, support functionality, understand site usage, and help protect the site. The exact technologies depend on the tools enabled on the live website.

You can usually control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect certain website features. If analytics or advertising tools are enabled, disclose the relevant tools and provide any consent controls required by applicable law.

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no website, transmission method, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access to personal information should be limited to people and providers who need it for legitimate business purposes and are expected to handle it appropriately.

Your information, your choices.

You may also choose not to provide optional information, although we may then be unable to respond fully to a service request. Contact us using the details below to make a privacy-related request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.
