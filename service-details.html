Exterior Detailing

Bring back a clean, deep finish with a meticulous exterior treatment focused on the surfaces that make the first impression.

Our exterior detailing process carefully cleans the bodywork, wheels, tyres, glass, and accessible trim. Each step is designed to lift everyday grime while treating your vehicle's finish with care.

What's included

  • Pre-wash and safe hand wash
  • Wheel, tyre, and exterior glass cleaning
  • Surface drying and finishing
  • Exterior trim and final inspection
Estimated time: 2–3 hours Fresh, refined finish

02 / INTERIOR CARE

Interior Detailing

Refresh the cabin with focused cleaning that helps your vehicle feel cleaner, more comfortable, and ready for the next drive.

From high-touch surfaces to the areas that collect dust and debris, interior detailing is tailored to your cabin materials and condition. Delicate finishes are treated with appropriate care.

What's included

  • Vacuuming seats, carpets, and mats
  • Dashboard and console cleaning
  • Door panels and interior glass
  • Material-appropriate surface treatment
Estimated time: 2–4 hours Cabin refresh

03 / SURFACE PROTECTION
GLOSS THAT LASTS

Ceramic Coating

Add a sleek, glossy finish and a durable protective layer with a coating service prepared and applied with precision.

Paint preparation is essential to a quality coating result. We assess the surface, clean it thoroughly, and apply the selected coating according to its product requirements. Protection lifespan depends on the product, preparation, driving conditions, and aftercare.

What's included

  • Thorough wash and surface preparation
  • Paint condition assessment
  • Careful coating application
  • Curing guidance and aftercare advice
Estimated time: 1–2 days Gloss and surface protection
Book Ceramic Coating
04 / PAINT REFINEMENT
CLARITY THROUGH PRECISION

Paint Correction

Improve paint clarity and gloss through a measured correction process designed around your vehicle's finish and condition.

After inspecting the paint, we select an appropriate polishing approach to reduce the appearance of selected swirl marks, oxidation, and light surface defects. Results vary by paint condition; deeper scratches may not be safely removable.

What's included

  • Paint inspection and defect assessment
  • Test spot and correction plan
  • Machine polishing where suitable
  • Final finish inspection
Estimated time: 1–2 days Tailored paint refinement
